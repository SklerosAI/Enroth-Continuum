# MM6 HUD navigation resources

Status: **MM6 frame, baked controls, time/status backgrounds and vital bars staged; linked checks and visual acceptance pending** (2026-11-06).

The inherited `SetUserInterface` skin requests many MM7 alignment-specific icons that are absent from the private MM6 icon archive. `UI_Create` likewise requests MM7 tome and status art. The retained archive contains a compass strip and two zoom-labelled controls whose names and dimensions make them plausible HUD candidates. The exact plus/minus meaning remains provisional. `hudNavigationResources(GameTitle, ...)` selects those three MM6 identifiers for every alignment while passing through the exact existing MM7 identifiers. `SetUserInterface` uses the result at its three alignment branches. A headless test checks that all three chosen MM6 entries can be read, and a pure test checks the title selection and exact MM7 neutral, good and evil preservation; neither test establishes zoom semantics.

The owner's original manual, page 30, shows a 640×480 adventure screen with a right panel, bottom portrait strip, top edge and left edge. Four retained MM6 frame images match those regions and dimensions: the right image ends at the bottom of the screen when placed at (468, 141), the portrait strip ends at the bottom at (0, 371), and the top and left edges meet at (0, 0) and (0, 8). `hudFrameAssets(GameTitle, ...)` selects those four images only for MM6, while `hudFramePositions(GameTitle)` selects their matching draw positions. The MM7 assets and positions pass through exactly for neutral, good and evil. Headless checks assert the four MM6 image dimensions and pure checks assert both title branches. No original image bytes are committed.

The right frame has four book images baked into it. The manual identifies four portable books: Quests, Notes, Map and Events. Its next page says the Events Book manages time; the retained MM6 icon archive has a time-oriented book background, and the current Calendar view displays time, day, month, year, moon and location. This supports routing the fourth **input** to the existing Calendar action, while its MM6 page content and art remain unverified.

`UI_Create` now installs four MM6 hit boxes over the baked book region, with matching Quest, Notes, Map and Calendar input actions. It does not request the absent MM7 tome icons or create an MM6 History button. MM7 still constructs its original five image-backed buttons. The MM6 books use no separate overlay or flashing texture until matching art exists; their visible spines remain in the right frame. Headless checks inspect each live button rectangle, action and message, the absence of the fifth button, and title isolation. Synthetic button delivery into a book screen cannot be accepted before the guarded first map is playable.

The MM6 History button pointer is explicitly null. The only route that stores it is the inherited Journal overlay constructor; that constructor does not dereference it, and the overlay's MM6 `Update` returns before accessing its button. The right-panel item's MM6 path also returns before touching the null storyline texture. A source search found no other pointer consumers. This makes the absent fifth button safe at the current shared call sites, while Journal content itself remains an unresolved MM6 route.

The hit boxes are bounded candidates measured against the retained frame; a graphical pass must verify cursor targeting and click feedback. The remaining minimap frame, status art and other skin requests stay explicit presentation blockers. The similarly named MM6 `book` item art belongs to spell-book inventory entries and is not evidence for HUD tomes. Resource presence alone does not establish a usable MM6 in-game interface; no detailed private icon inventory is tracked.

The same right frame has four baked lower controls. The original manual identifies their left-to-right actions as Cast Spell, Rest, Quick Reference and Game Options. `UI_Create` now creates MM6-only textureless hit boxes over those four visible ovals at (490, 398), (524, 398), (559, 398) and (594, 398), each 29×61. The existing messages and keyboard actions are retained. `OnButtonClick` skips the absent pressed image only for these four MM6 buttons, while still playing the click sound; the MM7 image-backed path is unchanged. The hit-box bounds are image-derived candidates, so cursor alignment and click feedback require a graphical pass. A headless test checks live button metadata, nonoverlap and absence of separate MM6 action textures. The frame-presence test supplies the underlying art; the available `buttcast` image does not establish four independent pressed states.

The retained MM6 time background is 360×300 and matches the Calendar/Events page role. The Calendar view now loads it only for MM6; MM7 continues loading `sbdate-time`. Headless and pure tests check its availability and title selection. The current Calendar text coordinates, title wording, moon icons and the full meaning of the MM6 Events page still need review and graphical acceptance.

The retained `arrowl*`/`arrowr*` sequence is used by MM6 party creation, not in-game NPC scrolling. A separate [UI resource audit](MM6_F4_UI_RESOURCE_BLOCKERS.md) identified the visibly opposing 14×25 `npc_lup`/`npc_rup` pair as the follower arrows and routed only those resource requests through a title-local helper. The inherited MM7 alignment names are preserved. NPC-scroll hit boxes, pressed-state icons and click feedback remain a presentation blocker.

The MM6 archive has a 483×24 footer with a text inset that matches the status strip consumer. `hudStatusBarIcon` selects it for MM6 and passes through the MM7 neutral, good and evil footer names unchanged. The current draw origin and text coordinates are retained until a graphical alignment check. Headless tests check the MM6 size and title isolation. The eight existing `MAPDIR` lookups also resolve to 7×7 MM6 icons; a focused presence test records that already working minimap subfamily.

The 172×142 MM6 top-panel images vary by visible environmental and alert states, while the inherited minimap frame is one alignment-specific image. Choosing one top image without mapping those states would misrepresent the original interface, so minimap-frame selection remains gated.

The MM6 portrait frame has two narrow vertical slots beside each character. Retained green hit and blue mana fills are each 6×78. The original MM6 executable's 16-bit HUD draw at `0x417C10` reads a four-character offset table at `0x4BD0FC` containing **0, 113, 226, 338**. Its hit destination adds 186 bytes (**x93**) and its mana destination adds 204 bytes (**x102**) to the framebuffer pointer. Both use row-offset table address `0x4CAD14`, which is **383 rows** beyond the renderer's `0x4CA718` row-table base. Thus the native fill rectangles are hit x93/206/319/431, mana x102/215/328/440, all y383 and 6×78. The earlier read-only art overlay's x94/209/323/439 and y385 estimate is superseded. The retained 468×109 `border2.pcx` frame is drawn at (0,371) and already contains the empty slot backdrops and caps; no separate empty-bar geometry is needed. The matching MM6 hover/status hit boxes now cover the corrected fill rectangles. MM7 retains its original resource names, color thresholds, draw offsets and hit boxes.

The focused MM6 hit-box and title-isolation tests passed **2/2** in a monitored headless run (69 samples, no on-screen window or focus change). A before/after hidden OpenGL capture passed **1/1** under the same no-window/no-focus guard (66 samples). Pixel comparison found **4,804 changed pixels**, all inside the eight bar areas, with no world, portrait or other HUD changes. The corrected fills visually sit inside the baked slots. A separate MM7 headless inventory control passed **1/1** (20 samples, no window/focus change). Hover response and character portrait hit boxes still need their own interaction check; the broad outdoor terrain artifact remains unrelated to these coordinates.

After the save/chest map gates open, a graphical pass must inspect the frame seams, compass crop and heading, zoom button meaning and feedback, all four book and action controls and their opened pages, follower arrow hit boxes and the surrounding HUD at the actual render resolution. Resolve the remaining icon families from source evidence and then verify them visually before claiming an MM6 HUD.
