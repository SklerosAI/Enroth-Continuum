# MM6 standard item bonus index map — F4 source join

Status, 2026-11-06: the source-backed 14-entry MM6 standard-bonus index map is centralized in `MM6StandardBonusMap.h` and used by `ItemTable::InitializeMm6`. No enchanted raw-item projection or live save route is opened by this change.

The pinned [MMExtension MM6 item layout](https://github.com/GrayFace/MMExtension/blob/9d73c0ff0482bd231ea522f752f4fe9b26640925/Scripts/Structs/01%20common%20structs.lua#L508-L529) labels the `Bonus` word as one plus an MM6 `Stats` value. Its pinned [MM6 Stats constants](https://github.com/GrayFace/MMExtension/blob/9d73c0ff0482bd231ea522f752f4fe9b26640925/Scripts/Core/ConstAndBits.lua#L3643-L3697) put the seven core stats at zero through six, HP/SP/armor at seven through nine, and fire/electricity/cold/poison resistance at ten through thirteen. The fork's MM6 item-table loader already used the corresponding 14 retained standard-enchantment rows. Centralizing that array removes an independent mapping that could drift when a raw-item view is added.

The shared runtime names MM6 electricity as air resistance, cold as water resistance and poison as body resistance. The map therefore sends original one-based bonus indices 12, 13 and 14 to `ATTRIBUTE_RESIST_AIR`, `ATTRIBUTE_RESIST_WATER` and `ATTRIBUTE_RESIST_BODY`. It deliberately has no index for inherited earth or mind resistance. The reverse function accepts only these 14 attributes; zero, index 15 and unsupported attributes reject. A focused unit test covers every forward/reverse index and the resistance edges.

The rebuilt private-backed MM6/LOD aggregate passed **352/352**, the rebuilt non-MM6-prefix unit suite **438/438**, and a monitored MM6 loaded-table headless check **1/1** over 64 samples with no on-screen window or focus change. The [plain-item projection](MM6_F4_PLAIN_ITEM_PROJECTION.md) still rejects nonzero bonus words. Next: prove bonus-strength bounds, category eligibility, effect application and mutation against the accepted source, then add a raw-preserving enchanted-item adapter before live item ownership and save/reload.
