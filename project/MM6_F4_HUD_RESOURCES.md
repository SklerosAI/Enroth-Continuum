# MM6 HUD navigation resources

Status: **compass/zoom and four frame candidates; headless resource checks pending; visual acceptance pending** (2026-11-06).

The inherited `SetUserInterface` skin requests many MM7 alignment-specific icons that are absent from the private MM6 icon archive. `UI_Create` likewise requests MM7 tome and status art. The retained archive contains a compass strip and two zoom-labelled controls whose names and dimensions make them plausible HUD candidates. The exact plus/minus meaning remains provisional. `hudNavigationResources(GameTitle, ...)` selects those three MM6 identifiers for every alignment while passing through the exact existing MM7 identifiers. `SetUserInterface` uses the result at its three alignment branches. A headless test checks that all three chosen MM6 entries can be read, and a pure test checks the title selection and exact MM7 neutral, good and evil preservation; neither test establishes zoom semantics.

The owner's original manual, page 30, shows a 640×480 adventure screen with a right panel, bottom portrait strip, top edge and left edge. Four retained MM6 frame images match those regions and dimensions: the right image ends at the bottom of the screen when placed at (468, 141), the portrait strip ends at the bottom at (0, 371), and the top and left edges meet at (0, 0) and (0, 8). `hudFrameAssets(GameTitle, ...)` selects those four images only for MM6, while `hudFramePositions(GameTitle)` selects their matching draw positions. The MM7 assets and positions pass through exactly for neutral, good and evil. Headless checks assert the four MM6 image dimensions and pure checks assert both title branches. No original image bytes are committed.

The right frame has four book images baked into it. The manual identifies four portable books: Quests, Notes, Map and Events. The inherited `UI_Create` constructs five tome buttons and draws separate MM7 tome icons. Their hit boxes and which route should serve MM6's Events book still need a title-specific decision before enabling visible MM6 book interaction. The remaining minimap frame, status art and other skin requests also stay explicit presentation blockers. The similarly named MM6 `book` item art belongs to spell-book inventory entries and is not evidence for HUD tomes. Resource presence alone does not establish a usable MM6 in-game interface; no detailed private icon inventory is tracked.

After the save/chest map gates open, a graphical pass must inspect the frame seams, compass crop and heading, zoom button meaning and feedback, all four book controls, and the surrounding HUD at the actual render resolution. Resolve the other icon families from source evidence and then verify them visually before claiming an MM6 HUD.
