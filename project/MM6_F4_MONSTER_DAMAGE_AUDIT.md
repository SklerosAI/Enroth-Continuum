# MM6 F4 monster damage label gate

Status: **typed parser and bounded effect gate, 2026-11-06**. The retained `monsters.txt` used here is part of the accepted F3-matched source set. This packet refines the [F2 combat audit](MM6_COMBAT_SEMANTICS_F2.md) for the title-specific parser and resistance lane.

Update, 2026-11-07: the claim below that consumers reject before damage changes was wrong at the time. The section [Ordering and character channels](#ordering-and-character-channels-2026-11-07) records the corrected preflight order and the opened character Electricity and Cold channels.

The table has 173 monster rows and 39 active secondary attacks, giving 212 active attack-type cells. Their labels aggregate as 130 physical, 21 fire, 17 electric, 10 cold, 12 poison, 12 energy and 10 magic. The inherited `ParseAttackType` recognizes the physical/fire labels but silently returns physical for the 49 electric, cold, poison and magic cells. It maps all 12 energy cells to Earth because of an inherited collision. The 134 `0` secondary-type placeholders have zero secondary chance and are outside the active 212. The MM6 loader now parses seven distinct typed labels, rejects an unknown **active** label, and ignores the zero-chance placeholder. The MM7 parser and its trace behavior remain unchanged.

The MM6 resistance table uses fire, electricity, cold, poison, magic and physical channels; current monster loading maps the four elemental columns onto inherited Fire/Air/Water/Earth fields. The typed parser stores electric/cold/poison in those inherited fields provisionally, and keeps Magic and Energy distinct. The shared character and actor damage consumers now reject MM6 Air/Water/Earth/Magic/Energy before changing damage or conditions. Physical and Fire retain their existing direct paths. In particular, an MM6 attack with one of the five gated labels cannot silently become physical or unresisted damage. This broad consumer gate also rejects other unverified MM6 damage types, including inherited non-elemental spell-school types; the separate spell-effect gate remains in force.

The gate is deliberately incomplete as gameplay: character MM6 resistance selection, the original numeric reduction formula, Magic and Energy channel semantics, and the relationship of Energy to the six resistance columns remain unverified. The stored `Imm` marker still maps to 200 in monster fields, but that does not establish original MM6 immunity behavior. No MM6 encounter or original installation was opened for this change. Focused native tests cover all seven labels, the Energy/Earth collision, unknown active labels, zero-chance placeholders, and MM6-versus-MM7 effect gating. A private retained-table count reconfirmed all 212 active cells and 134 placeholders.

The loader now also keeps an explicit MM6-only six-column resistance view beside the inherited MM7-shaped fields. The view names Fire, Electricity, Cold, Poison, Physical and Magic separately; it does not invent an Energy column. A typed lookup rejects Energy and missing MM6 source data. An offline native check loaded the retained 173-row table and matched the six `Imm` aggregates (18, 14, 13, 45, 5 and 54) to the F2 audit. This is source-value preservation, not a change to live resistance arithmetic or an immunity proof. The damage gate above remains in force.

Next F4 checks: verify character and monster resistance selection, secondary attack chance/order, original numeric damage and immunity behavior; then enable each gated channel with offline state tests. Cover each label in offline damage scenarios and a mixed-resistance F5 encounter. Keep MM7 parsing and trace behavior separate.

## Ordering and character channels, 2026-11-07

The earlier claim that rejection happens before damage changes did not hold. `DamageMonsterFromParty` checked the Earth channel for stun and paralysis after hit points, death and experience had changed, and the special-attack guard ran after `receiveDamage`. Party attacks, monster hits on characters and monster-on-monster hits now run an MM6 preflight over every spell, channel and special attack the call can reach, before the monster, the party or the RNG changes.

Characters now take Electricity and Cold as well as Physical and Fire. A hash-checked read of the original executable's character damage routine shows that physical damage passes unchanged, and that each elemental channel sums the character's base and bonus resistance, item and magic bonuses and the hired Enchanter bonus, then halves the damage up to four times while a roll against luck bonus plus resistance plus 30 reaches 30. Unlike MM7 the roll runs even when luck and resistance add up to nothing. The shared `GetActualResistance` already sums the same terms for MM6 characters. Monster-side Electricity and Cold, Poison, Magic and Energy remain closed. Event damage keeps the old two-channel gate, because the MM6 event operand is an original damage kind with no verified mapping to `DamageType`.
