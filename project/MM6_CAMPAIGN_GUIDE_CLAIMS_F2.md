## Method and denominator

The owner-retained [Prima scan](https://www.mocagh.org/nwc/mm6-hintbook.pdf) has 412 PDF pages. Its contents pages 7–8 identify **8 essential-quest headings**, **12 class-promotion headings plus the Seer** (13 important-quest headings), **10 regional minor-quest groups plus an additional-puzzles group** (11 groups), and a map appendix. The guide's printed page is PDF page minus eight in these chapters. I visually inspected PDF pp. **265, 267, 283, 295, 312, 325, 346 and 347** to check the stable/ship tables, opening and Oracle claims, promotion/Seer framing, and the outdoor map grid. The remaining headings were indexed from the contents and OCR text, not visually or semantically confirmed one by one. The private ledger identifies every heading and its evidence tier.

The retained `quest-graph-probe.json`, `campaign-routes-probe.json`, `house-route-probe.json`, `map-index.tsv`, and `2devents.txt` were compared with current consumers at source SHA `9f07d308d`. They yield structural candidates, not a proof that any quest branch can execute. The existing keyword graph covers 208 script-used quest bits, but its six milestone groups do not exhaust the 8+13+11 guide headings. Every heading still needs an exact quest-bit/event/NPC/item/map chain or an evidenced exception before F2 freeze.
