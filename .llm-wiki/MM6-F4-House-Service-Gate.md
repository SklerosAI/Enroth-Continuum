# MM6 F4 House Service Gate

The [F4 packet](../project/MM6_F4_HOUSE_SERVICE_GATE.md) explains the title-specific entry gate and the retained house-type/range audit. The MM6 classifier records shop, magic-guild, stable/boat and other families without indexing MM7 house-ID arrays. All MM6 house entry still rejects before inherited room media, shop timers, NPC mutation or service windows. MM7 keeps its current path.

The ignored format-2 house-route report keeps exact row/event IDs and hashes. The next agent needs the MM6 room descriptor table, title-specific stock keys and refresh state, service windows and offline event-to-house scenarios before narrowing this gate. A parser category or an in-range room ID does not prove a working house.
