## OpenEnroth

Enroth Continuum is a fork of [OpenEnroth](https://github.com/OpenEnroth/OpenEnroth),
the open-source reimplementation of the Might & Magic VI-VIII engine.

- Upstream repository: <https://github.com/OpenEnroth/OpenEnroth>
- Upstream organization: [OpenEnroth](https://github.com/OpenEnroth)
- Upstream license: LGPL-3.0-or-later; the unmodified upstream `LICENSE` is retained

- Upstream authors and contributors retain credit for their work. This fork preserves
  upstream Git history so that authorship remains visible and traceable.

OpenEnroth is the technical foundation of this project. SklerosAI does not claim to
be the original author of the engine or of the Might & Magic games.

## Original game rights

Might & Magic VI, VII, and VIII and their original game data are not part of this
repository. Players must supply their own lawfully obtained game installations. Do
not commit, publish, attach to releases, or otherwise redistribute original game
assets, installers, saves containing game assets, music, art, dialogue, videos, or
archives through this project.

## Fork-specific work

Any SklerosAI-authored changes must identify themselves in commit messages, release
notes, and relevant source headers or documentation. When a change is broadly useful
to OpenEnroth, prefer an upstream contribution or keep the fork delta small enough
to make that contribution practical.
