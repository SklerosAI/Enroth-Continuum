# MM6 plain character inventory projection — F4 detached stage

Status, 2026-11-06: **superseded as the current API** by the [bounded inventory projection](MM6_F4_BOUNDED_INVENTORY_PROJECTION.md). This packet records the initial plain-only `stagePlainInventory`/`capturePlainInventory` stage and its accepted checks; those names were replaced by `stageBoundedInventory`/`captureBoundedInventory`, which also admit standard-enchanted equipment. Neither stage attaches a raw owner to Party or normal SaveLoad.

The MM6 physical region has 138 item records, a 126-word backpack grid and 16 equipment words. The stage takes an explicit MM6 title and the same loaded `ItemTable` used by the runtime. Each positive grid/equipment reference must name a unique in-range source record; the item's ID, template and supported fields must validate. The runtime places it at the same record index and grid anchor or equipment slot. Rebuilding the entire grid and equipment tables from that staged runtime must reproduce every original word, including negative continuation cells. Unknown or unreferenced item records remain in a private raw sidecar. The projection's source and original placement metadata are private to the staged object, so a caller cannot forge a new raw owner through its public inventory accessor.

Capture starts from those raw bytes. It requires the same active record set, original MM6 table types, grid/equipment zone and equipment slot, then applies only the [plain-item projection's](MM6_F4_PLAIN_ITEM_PROJECTION.md) bounded field updates. A backpack item may move to a different grid anchor with its exact source index retained. Missing, new, newly equipped or re-equipped records, or a changed template type, reject instead of inventing item body-location or disposal semantics. Normal item consumption, transfer to another character, loot, potions, enchantments and full saves are still open.

Focused linked raw-item, raw-inventory, plain-item and plain-inventory checks passed **8/8**. They cover the final record index, equipment reference, negative continuation, unreferenced raw tail, malformed indexes, unsupported bonus union, source-type isolation, supported flag update and rejection of removal or equipment-slot change. A monitored MM6 headless test used the loaded private-backed item table and actual icon footprint to stage and recapture a synthetic final-slot scroll; it passed **1/1** over 75 samples with no on-screen window or focus change. No private source bytes are tracked.
