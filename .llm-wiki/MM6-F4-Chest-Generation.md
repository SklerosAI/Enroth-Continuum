The MM6 chest grid/tail remains opaque. **Every MM6 level load reaching this call is blocked**, including marker-free maps, until a generator or proved skip policy exists.

The remap table is encoded in a detached tested helper; **the live guard remains closed**. The item picker branches over MM6 1–399 regular templates and 400–429 tier-6 artifacts, then potion/wand/enchantment fields. The direct tier-7 picker appears to include candidate 430, beyond its owned-ID scan. Placement calls separate fit/finalize helpers. Next agents should read the ignored private packet `local-game-data/private-wiki/mm6/F4_CHEST_PE_TRACE_2026-11-06.md` and bounded disassembly under `local-game-data/mm6/research-2026-11-06/`. Complete the nested RNG/field and placement contracts, then test all six active markers, artifact state, full chests and save/reload before lifting the guard. MM7 must keep its own generator. The first detached stage, `mm6::pickChestBaseItemId`, models the **ANY base-ID stage only**. It preserves the original 15-bit RNG, unconditional artifact-candidate draw, tier-6 5%/13-cap trial, and the zero-remainder choice of item 1 even at a zero-weight tier. Six retained `rnditems.txt` weight sums match the expected bounded 1–399 domain. Seeded tests cover artifact success/fallback, original weighted boundaries and MM7 rejection. It does not construct an item or publish state.

The second detached stage, `mm6::finalizeChestAnyItem`, models only field writes made by that original **ANY picker** after base selection. It sets the identification flag from the template, draws weapon/equipment enchantment chance and weighted fields as eligible, and sets wand current charges from the template byte plus `RNG % 6`. A plain potion gains no power and consumes no additional draw at this stage; chest placement and other item sources require separate tracing. Standard attribute remainder zero can choose a zero-weight first attribute. Tier-six artifact success exits without regular field writes. Caller rows are preflighted, and the function returns staged fields/flags/RNG without publishing state. Seeded native tests cover these branches and malformed rows. The private packet records exact PE branch evidence. The live chest guard stays closed until original 28-byte item construction, placement, grid/tail encoding and save/reload are proved.
