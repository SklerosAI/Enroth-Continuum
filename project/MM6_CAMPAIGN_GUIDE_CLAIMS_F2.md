## Method and denominator

The owner-retained [Prima scan](https://www.mocagh.org/nwc/mm6-hintbook.pdf) has 412 PDF pages. Its contents pages 7–8 identify **8 essential-quest headings**, **12 class-promotion headings plus the Seer** (13 important-quest headings), **10 regional minor-quest groups plus an additional-puzzles group** (11 groups), and a map appendix. The guide's printed page is PDF page minus eight in these chapters. I visually inspected PDF pp. **265, 267, 283, 295, 312, 325, 346 and 347** to check the stable/ship tables, opening and Oracle claims, promotion/Seer framing, and the outdoor map grid. The remaining headings were indexed from the contents and OCR text, not visually or semantically confirmed one by one. The private ledger identifies every heading and its evidence tier.

The retained `quest-index.tsv`, `item-index.tsv`, `map-index.tsv`, `npcdata.txt`, `quest-graph-probe.json`, `campaign-routes-probe.json`, `house-route-probe.json`, `transport-fields-probe.json`, and `2devents.txt` were compared with current consumers. They yield structural candidates, not a proof that any quest branch can execute. The existing graph covers 208 script-used quest bits, but its six keyword milestone groups do not exhaust the 8+13+11 guide headings.

## Per-heading candidate join status

The private JSON records **all 32 guide headings/groups** individually with printed/PDF locator in its companion ledger, retained quest-bit, event-function, NPC, item and map candidates where identified, a confidence level, the unresolved behavior, and a per-heading F4/F5 acceptance check. **28** have one or more directly named retained quest-note candidates with structural event read/write links; **3** have map/NPC candidates but no exact quest-note row; **1** additional-puzzles group has no single retained join. **13** headings also have named item-template candidates. The 12 class-promotion headings each have a distinct quest-note candidate and a corresponding promotion-giver NPC candidate. The six High Council subquests have six matching quest-note leads; the four Oracle crystals have four note/item leads. These are candidate joins, not decoded event control flow, complete individual regional quest enumeration, or implemented rewards. The Seer has a group of 25 note-labelled bits, not a verified twelve-month state machine.
