# MM6 Guide F2 Review Source

The [guide review ledger](../project/MM6_GUIDE_F2_REVIEW.md) maps the owner's retained manual, reference card and Prima scan into 14 public mechanic/campaign families with current C++ consumers and F3/F4/F5 acceptance scenarios. The tracked [guide coverage probe](../scripts/mm6_probe_guide_coverage.py) verifies source PDF identities and indexes pages; OCR-light pages are inspection flags, not missing pages. Chapter locations and selected visually checked claims are useful leads, but the full guide has **not** been reconciled sentence by sentence with retained data and engine behavior.

The ledger distinguishes a documented or source-backed fact, an unresolved interpretation, and a current engine path without demonstrated MM6 support. Creation bounds, potion chart disagreement, class/skill and spell inventories, media/house gaps, map and campaign candidates are cross-linked to their focused packets. Exact guide wording, page-level notes, source hashes, table rows and disagreements remain in ignored local research. A guide locator is not proof that a route executes or that its stated numeric rule is the shipped executable's behavior.
