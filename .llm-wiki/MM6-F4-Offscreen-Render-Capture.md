# MM6 F4 Offscreen Render Capture

The [public packet](../project/MM6_F4_OFFSCREEN_FRAME_CAPTURE.md) records the test-only hidden SDL OpenGL contract. The `MM6OffscreenFrame.DISABLED_FirstOutdoorCapture` GameTest is explicitly opt-in, isolated in a fresh process and restricted to a normalized temporary PNG path. `OffscreenCaptureGuard` blocks native show/raise, cursor and mouse changes, and modal boxes while leaving the SDL OpenGL context usable. The normal MM6 and MM7 launch paths and the null-renderer suite do not select this mode.

The same hidden test can optionally capture a first-open MM6 chest to a second normalized temporary PNG. It prefers an item-bearing untrapped chest, draws through the MM6 chest entry/GUI, checks a substantial pixel difference from the outdoor frame, and closes the GUI before teardown. The integrated monitored run passed 1/1 with no visible window or focus change; visual inspection showed the chest panel and item icons in the expected region. The private PNG is untracked. This does not validate trap, pickup, save/reload or campaign behavior.

The first hidden draw reached a retained MM6 seven-character directional sprite prefix and aborted because inherited `Sprites.cpp` did not append its octant. Retained `dsft.bin` and sprite-archive aggregate evidence supports appending at seven for MM6: 981 eligible prefixes had numbered zero-octant entries and none had an exact unsuffixed entry. MM7's existing `<7` condition is retained. A focused MM6 headless test passed 1/1 with eight nonnull octants; accepted executable control flow for this lookup was not independently recovered.

The initial hidden OpenGL capture passed 1/1 but showed black party portrait ovals because the test read pixels before `OpenGLRenderer::flushAndScale` submitted queued `DrawQuad2D` portrait vertices. This supersedes the earlier claim that the black portraits were an unexplained runtime defect. The test now follows `Engine::Draw` order through HUD flush and overlay draw before readback. A four-face chromatic pixel assertion distinguishes the corrected result from the prior black ovals. The corrected capture passed 1/1 in a monitored arm64 process: 164 ten-millisecond samples, zero on-screen windows and no focus change. Its private 640×480 PNG was inspected locally and shows four colored portraits alongside first-map geometry and HUD. No screenshot or detailed proprietary asset inventory entered Git. A later capture identified and corrected MM6 sprite-frame field offsets; see below. Do not infer visual correctness, normal new-game entry or gameplay from this diagnostic capture.

The MM6 `dsft.bin` frame records use 16-bit flags, light radius at byte 46, palette at 48, frame time at 52 and total time at 54, as the [pinned MMExtension `SFTItem` schema](https://github.com/GrayFace/MMExtension/blob/a2ab9b12705de7576aecf8111168666d6398f830/Scripts/Structs/01%20common%20structs.lua#L2405-L2446) and retained file agree. The inherited MM7-style layout misread palette and timing. Separate 56-byte MM6 and 60-byte MM7 snapshots now preserve each title's layout. The retained `tree01` frame uses palette 140 and its art is green; `tree03` uses the same palette and its original art is red. A temporary draw trace found 65 visible tree billboards using palette 140. The corrected final hidden frame passed **1/1** (73 samples, no on-screen window or focus change), with green and red foliage in the expected locations. A focused MM6 palette/portrait test passed **2/2** (78 samples); an MM7 headless control passed **1/1** (32 samples), both without window or focus change. Private PNGs and asset extracts remain outside Git. The ground or water remains cyan/red and is a separate unresolved renderer/terrain path.

The earlier large white foreground disappeared with the separate MM6 water-source correction. A private pixel comparison found **19,399** near-white lower-world pixels in the old frame and **zero** in the new frame. The old logs requested missing inherited MM7 water/shore names, but this comparison alone does not prove which missing texture or array-size interaction produced every old white pixel. Only a small pale area beyond the gate remains; its exact model or texture owner is unproved. The current bright cyan/red ground is the separate tile-zero placeholder issue. The native MM6 vital-bar coordinates were also corrected in a later hidden capture; its before/after changed pixels were confined to the bar slots. See [HUD resources](MM6-F4-HUD-Resources.md).

The custom linked binary also passed the MM7 `Inventory.Initialization` headless control 1/1 (45 samples, no window/focus change). A wrong-filter `--offscreen-gl` invocation was rejected before SDL startup. Parent integration must still build and rerun the focused capture from the committed source.

The retained MM6 archive uses `wtrtyl` and `wtrdr*`, without MM7 `HDWTR` frames. A title-aware tile-table load keeps the original MM6 shore names while MM7 retains its hardware names; MM6 shore masks expose palette-zero transparency and the renderer sources its seven water layers from `wtrtyl` for MM6. Synthetic title isolation passed 1/1, and a hidden private diagnostic found matching CPU/GPU water-layer pixels. The earlier bright cyan/red-ring terrain was the engine's `pending` placeholder. The prior claim that the original map uses 209 tile-zero cells is superseded: the original grid has no local tile ID zero. The inherited loader discarded MM6's authored selector bases and produced 209 `pending` cells through lossy MM7 tileset normalization. The [selector page](MM6-F4-Outdoor-Tile-Selectors.md) records the original-executable trace and MM6-only fix. Black ground in the next capture came from MM6 land sharing water's GPU texture unit; the shader sampled an empty land unit. The integrated MM6 allocator correction passed a hidden recapture 1/1 over 130 samples with no window/focus change and shows land and road textures, with zero exact-black pixels in the measured ground region. The broad white foreground patch disappeared after the water-source correction, while small pale upper-center highlights remain unassigned. Complete visual fidelity is still open. See the [updated public packet](../project/MM6_F4_OFFSCREEN_FRAME_CAPTURE.md).

A fresh first-map capture passed 1/1 over 105 samples without a visible window or focus change. The current pale ground differs from that earlier missing-water patch: the diagnostic begins in month 0, the current outdoor loader applies seasonal terrain, and the current season map selects snow for grass in months 11, 0 and 1. A temporary terrain-only image showed pale ground before buildings or HUD. Winter remapping is therefore the likely source of these pixels, but per-pixel tile ownership and original MM6 seasonal behavior remain unproved. No runtime change was made. A later isolated diagnostic's monitor saw a final frontmost-app change, so further graphical runs stopped and temporary files were removed.
