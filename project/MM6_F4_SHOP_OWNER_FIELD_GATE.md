# MM6 `SHOP` owner field gate — F4

Status, 2026-11-06: a detached, raw physical `SHOP` section now preserves the original contiguous stock region, but **no `SHOP` field is safe to add to the required known-save manifest or live `.ec6` transaction**. The only joined shop/guild owner field is the keyed 64-bit refresh deadline encoded in required `SCLK` v1. Normal MM6 SaveLoad and house services remain guarded.

## Evidence and decision

The accepted original-executable trace and retained 557-row house matrix establish the active house row as the stock and refresh key. Shop rows 1–47 and spell-guild rows 119–140 have proved refresh-deadline reads/writes; the final shop and first guild address the same physical deadline word. `ShopGuildClockFields` preserves those **68 distinct words**, tests nonzero round-trip and rejects late truncation without mutating its destination. This is a complete bounded field, already implemented; adding a second copy under `SHOP` would create two authorities for one physical word.

| Candidate new field | What the source proves | Missing for a complete owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One shop or guild stock item ID | Both shop banks and the guild display scan a 12-position physical stride of 28-byte records and skip a slot whose first 32-bit item ID is zero. The successful purchase path clears that ID as part of bytes 0–24. | The remaining 24 bytes are not typed. A nonzero ID alone would discard enchantments, charges or other item state and cannot represent a full stocked slot. |
| Raw 28-byte stock record | Physical record stride, original raw `party.bin` inclusion and the last-shop/first-guild second-bank **physical record alias** are established. Successful purchase copies all 28 bytes to inventory, then clears bytes 0–24 of the stock record. The keyed index and detached purchase helpers preserve those facts. | Full item field semantics, the next read and how the two services interact at the shared record over refresh remain unproved. A blind pair of 47-row arrays would duplicate one physical slot. The fork has no MM6-keyed runtime owner or service writer to keep an opaque record current. |
| Ban, in-shop or bounty tuple | The inherited MM7 code has analogous state families. | Its `HouseId` intervals do not identify MM6 spell guilds or candidate town halls. Original MM6 row keys, widths, lifetime and write semantics for these fields are unproved. |

The existing partial session coordinator intentionally rejects nonempty inherited MM7-indexed shop/guild/bounty arrays. The source-proven stock address formulas have a title-explicit physical-record-index helper: bank one and bank two accept shop rows 1–47 and positions 0–11; guild rows 119–140 share the second bank's final-shop 12 positions. A detached purchase helper models full-record transfer and the original partial source clear. The new detached `ShopStockFields` allocates one raw 1,440-record physical array and can round-trip it as isolated `SHOP` v1, but it has no live Party capture or service writer. The [purchase-write follow-up](MM6_F4_SHOP_PURCHASE_WRITE_GATE.md) also records an independent editor layout that starts the guild list one physical row later than the original active guild address. Therefore **no required `SHOP` section, provisional item-ID field or MM7 array adapter is added**.

## Shortest unlock

1. Trace the next display read across purchase and refresh at the shared physical row; verify the original record layout and alias against an owner-local nonzero `party.bin` sample from the user's owned game data. The successful purchase write itself is now traced for all three banks.
2. Connect a title-local live Party owner to the proved physical array with a single policy for every shared slot. Have MM6 service generation and purchase paths write that owner; validate every nonzero item template against the loaded MM6 item table. Keep bans and bounty separate until their own row and lifetime traces are complete.
3. Only then add `SHOP` to the mandatory known-save section set. Stage all rows on a copy, reject invalid full item records and inject failure at the final row to prove that neither the live owner nor the previous disk slot changes. Verify save–quit–reload after all remaining `.ec6` owners and world commit are ready.

## Checks performed

The ignored owner-local stock checker revalidated the accepted executable identity, retained shop/guild row bands, bank/stride/display reads, family-edge arithmetic, raw Party save span and current cross-map autosave order. It passed on 2026-11-06. The retained active-house matrix has two `SpeakInHouse` references to the final General Store row and four to the first Fire Guild row. The observed shop and guild generation paths use those house rows, and the second-bank and guild address formulas coincide at all 12 positions; that establishes a physical identity, while service conflict behavior remains open. The previously integrated `MM6ShopGuildClockFields.*` suite passed **3/3**; the later purchase and detached stock-field cases await linked integration. The new stock codec's standalone native nonzero round-trip and malformed-load check passed. No native MM6 save sample, game process or proprietary bytes were added to Git.
