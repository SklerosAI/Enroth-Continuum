The retained rows aggregate to 47 shop types, 22 magic-guild types, 21 stable/boat types and 467 other types. Mercenary and thieves guilds are in Other because their behavior does not fit the spellbook guild helper. Wagon remains Other until its travel policy is proven. This classifier is a handoff for future title-specific services; it does not authorize the inherited shop stock, guild awards or refresh arrays.

`enterHouse` dispatches MM6 to `mm6EnterHouse` in `src/GUI/UI/Houses/MM6House.cpp`. It preflights the title-specific house table and private room manifest. A blank, unused or terminal entry, an unreadable table or manifest, or a missing proprietor icon or room movie refuses with a status line and returns before any house state changes. A valid entry opens the room movie, or the static proprietor portrait when video is off, and `mm6CreateHouseUI` builds the MM6 house screen. A room with no movie, no proprietor and no resident NPC still refuses. The screen lists the proprietor and the NPCs whose house is this one. The proprietor lists the services of `mm6::houseServiceOptions`, keyed by service family and house type. NPCs list their scripted topics, which run their global events. The transport menu remains an offline seam and is not listed on the house screen.
