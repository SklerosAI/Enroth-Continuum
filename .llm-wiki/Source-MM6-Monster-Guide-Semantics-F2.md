# MM6 Monster Guide Semantics F2 Source

The [monster catalog packet](../project/MM6_MONSTER_GUIDE_SEMANTICS_F2.md) gives all 173 retained monster IDs an evidence status: 90 direct page-image identities, 68 exact OCR names on expected catalog pages, 13 page-order candidates and two outside the printed 57-group catalog. These statuses identify guide entries, not sprites, spawn behavior or live combat.

A new 51-variant visual comparison found 15 differences among 204 selected level/HP/armor/XP cells and three differences among 51 treasure strings. The packet also ties selected guide labels to inherited parser fallbacks for Energy, missiles, poison and spells. Original shipped data must decide disputed values. The remaining catalog variants, C++ effects, AI, loot, resistance and graphical encounters are open. Exact rows, values and page captures stay ignored locally.
