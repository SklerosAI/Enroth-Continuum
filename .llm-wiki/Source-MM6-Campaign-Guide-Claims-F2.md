# MM6 Campaign Guide Claims F2 Source

The [campaign guide-claim packet](../project/MM6_CAMPAIGN_GUIDE_CLAIMS_F2.md) indexes eight essential quest headings, 13 promotion/Seer headings and 11 regional groups, with selected direct scan checks. Fifteen outdoor guide names join the retained map index by name. Four Oracle crystal quest-note bits have candidate global EVT writes, but their item handoff and branch behavior remain unproven. Exact headings and candidate joins remain private.

The guide shows nine stable and eleven ship itinerary rows; retained `2devents.txt` has nine stable and twelve boat houses. Route-like fields in that table are a candidate schedule source, but the current house loader ignores them and transport uses MM7 arrays. The extra boat and all route costs, days, coordinates and gates require original-data reconciliation and live checks. This packet does not prove quest reachability or playable travel.
