## Additional acceptance gaps and source cautions

1. **Input and item handling.** The card's keyboard diagram adds `Y` to yell, absent from the manual's keyboard table. The manual/card also specify item-in-hand right-click shortcuts, right-click potion mixing, direct spell targeting with Control-click, and a target crosshair fallback. The existing screen inventory names broad screen families but does not by itself prove those action routes. F4 should put these into the MM6 input/action map; F5 should test valid and invalid targets, including an empty bottle, scroll and spellbook.
2. **Social state and services.** The manual says shared fame never decreases, reputation drifts toward neutral, temple donations can improve it, and disreputable followers may affect it. The card adds NPC refusal with beg/threaten/bribe responses and a reputation cost. These are acceptance checks for the MM6 NPC, temple and time consumers, including save/reload; exact rates and gates require F3 evidence.
3. **Controls and options.** The card specifies separate music/effects/voice volumes, graphical detail, turn-rate choices, walking sounds and damage display, plus an autosave at the top of the load list after a level exit. F4 should establish the MM6-specific options and save policy. F5 should reopen settings, cross a map boundary, and verify visible slot order and durable state.
4. **Combat and spellbook interaction.** The manual describes independent character recovery, a slower recovery while moving/running, timing-gem color with an indoor caveat, melee-versus-bow-versus-pass fallback, and spellbook keyboard selection/readied-spell actions. These require interaction checks beyond a rendered battle or successful cast. The spell descriptions provide indoor/outdoor, daylight/moonlight, once-per-day and spell-point-drain cases for F5, while the [spell guide audit](MM6_SPELL_GUIDE_CLAIMS_F2.md) remains the per-ID comparison ledger.
5. **Conflicting manual prose.** PDF page 27 assigns Expert sword speed and Master sword offhand use, plus Expert dagger offhand use and Master dagger kill chance; page 40 reverses or changes those examples. PDF page 47 places Stone to Flesh under Earth magic but refers to Spirit skill for its duration. Page 40's experience example is too imprecise to define cumulative level thresholds. These should be adjudicated in F3 from original rule data and, where necessary, F5 behavior. Do not encode a selected sentence solely because it appears in the manual.
6. **Incomplete final spell claim.** PDF page 54 explicitly withholds operational detail for the final Dark spell. Its identity/effects must come from retained spell data and original behavior, not an inferred manual effect. The guide's own unresolved rank clauses are noted in the [spell guide audit](MM6_SPELL_GUIDE_CLAIMS_F2.md).

This completes **page-level F2 source coverage** for the manual and card, with the narrative and tiny/blank pages classified honestly. It does **not** close the F3 original-data preflight, F4 implementation, or F5 graphical and persistence acceptance.
