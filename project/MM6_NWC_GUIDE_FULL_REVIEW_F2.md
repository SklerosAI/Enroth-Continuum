# MM6 limited-edition guide: complete page-level F2 review

Status: **source-claim review, not original-file or runtime validation** (2026-11-05). The 1998 [New World Computing limited-edition strategy guide](https://www.mocagh.org/nwc/mm6le-strategyguide.pdf) is an 11-PDF-page scan of facing printed pages. All 11 PDF pages were directly viewed in a contact sheet; the potion pages, dungeon-map pages and skill chart received closer image/text inspection. Its PDF and exact per-page/claim ledgers are retained under ignored `local-game-data/mm6/research-2026-11-05/` as `mm6le-strategyguide.pdf`, `nwc-guide-page-ledger.tsv` and `nwc-guide-claims-private.json`. The private report fingerprints the scan, retained `skilldes.txt`, `map-index.tsv` and the existing `useitems.txt` probe. No original GOG installation, executable or archive was opened, and MM6 was not launched.
