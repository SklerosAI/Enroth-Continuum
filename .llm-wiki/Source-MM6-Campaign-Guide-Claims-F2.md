# MM6 Campaign Guide Claims F2 Source

The [campaign guide-claim packet](../project/MM6_CAMPAIGN_GUIDE_CLAIMS_F2.md) gives all 32 guide headings/groups a private candidate quest-bit/event/NPC/item/map join and acceptance scenario. Twenty-eight have named quest-note and structural EVT leads; all 12 promotions have distinct note candidates. Fifteen outdoor guide names join the retained map index by name. Four Oracle crystal quest-note bits have candidate global EVT writes, but their item handoff and branch behavior remain unproven. Exact headings and joins remain private.

The guide shows nine stable and eleven ship itinerary rows with 36 printed option cells; retained `2devents.txt` has nine stable and twelve boat houses with 37 populated cells. All printed cells were privately compared: 34 duration values agree and two differ. Route-like fields in the table are a candidate schedule source, but the current house loader ignores them and transport uses MM7 arrays. The extra boat, disputed durations, day tokens, destination grid, costs and gates require original-data reconciliation and live checks. This packet does not prove quest reachability or playable travel.
