# Project Overview

Enroth Continuum is SklerosAI's personal Apple Silicon fork of OpenEnroth, centered on locally owned Might & Magic VI, VII, and VIII installations.

The engine foundation, Git history, license, and authorship come from [OpenEnroth](https://github.com/OpenEnroth/OpenEnroth). Enroth Continuum adds the Atlas Mac launcher and title-specific work. [CREDITS.md](../CREDITS.md) and [project/UPSTREAM.md](../project/UPSTREAM.md) are the authority for attribution; this wiki does not replace them.

The intended result is three independently working campaign runtimes in one local installation, with separate private profiles and optional mods. Cross-game character transfer is a future opt-in feature, contingent on working source and destination games. The public roadmap distinguishes intent from verified implementation.
