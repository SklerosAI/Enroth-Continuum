# MM6 F4 Outdoor Border Trace

The [source packet](../project/MM6_F4_OUTDOOR_BORDER_TRACE.md) records a bounded original-MM6 border-route trace. Exact executable offsets and retained map names remain in ignored local evidence.

Original MM6 derives the neighbor from a five-column, three-row outdoor filename grid and out-of-bounds party coordinates. The accepted 15 outdoor map entries cover that grid. The offer caller checks three low Party flag bits for water damage, airborne and standing on water; burning is absent from this mask. Failure clamps the party inside the map. Confirmation separately refuses nonzero flying state, rechecks the neighbor without repeating the offer mask, applies a five-day baseline reduced by hireling checks with a one-day floor, and wraps the crossed axes by one full map width to preserve edge offset. Both axes may change at a corner. This differs from the MM7 border table and named arrival-point path.

Full confirmation dispatch, later party-state changes, corner behavior and a native nonzero arrival sample remain open. A pure [detached calculator](../src/Engine/MM6/MM6OutdoorBorderTravel.h) returns bounded grid/arrival/time candidates, and separate [eligibility predicates](../src/Engine/MM6/MM6OutdoorBorderEligibility.h) model the proved three-bit offer mask and zero-flying confirmation check. They read or mutate no Party/global state and still never authorize a live offer. Four synthetic route checks, four eligibility checks and three transition-intercept controls passed **11/11** on the integration build. The current MM6 title guard remains, and cross-map autosave still requires a complete `.ec6` transaction or separately proved transition policy. The traced helper has no direct fog or underwater predicate; this does not resolve MM6 weather.
