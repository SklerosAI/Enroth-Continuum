# MM6 Monster Guide Semantics F2 Source

The [monster catalog packet](../project/MM6_MONSTER_GUIDE_SEMANTICS_F2.md) gives all 173 retained monster IDs an evidence status. All 171 printed-catalog variants now have direct page-image identity and selected-field evidence; the final two are outside the 57-group guide catalog. This identifies guide entries, not sprites, spawn behavior or live combat.

For 132 variants in this packet, 50 of 528 selected level/HP/armor/XP cells and four of 132 treasure strings differ from retained data; the prior 39-variant packet used a different selected-field set. The packet also ties selected guide labels to inherited parser fallbacks for Energy, missiles, poison and spells. Original shipped data must decide disputed values. Full C++ effects, AI, loot, resistance, sprite/spawn joins and graphical encounters remain open. Exact rows, values and page captures stay ignored locally.
