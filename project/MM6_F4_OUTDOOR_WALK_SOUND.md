# MM6 F4 outdoor walking sounds

Status: **title-specific raw tileset dispatch staged; linked headless and audible acceptance pending** (2026-11-06).

The original MM6 executable was rehashed against the accepted F3 identity before a bounded read-only trace. Its outdoor movement caller reaches a terrain sound dispatcher at `0x46e380`. The dispatcher reconstructs the authored global tile ID from the local map selector, reads the tile-table's raw 16-bit tileset field, and selects a walking or running sound through a 28-entry table. All 28 active entries were checked against the implemented grouping; exact executable bytes and private table rows remain ignored locally.

The inherited engine asks `OutdoorTerrain::tilesetByPos()` for a normalized MM7-style `Tileset`. That enum collapses distinct MM6 terrain and road classes, so the walking sound can be wrong even when the visible tile ID is correct. `TileData` now retains its raw source tileset and variant alongside the normalized fields. The MM6 outdoor movement route dispatches walking/running sound from the retained raw tileset. Representative original classes include a distinct cooled-lava sound, road sound and ground sound. MM7 still uses its previous normalized `walkSoundForTileset` route. Unsupported raw IDs suppress a walking sound rather than indexing an unchecked entry; this intentionally fails closed for malformed or generated records.

A focused test checks raw identity survival through snapshot reconstruction, all 28 original dispatcher entries grouped by sound family, representative MM6 terrain/road calls, unsupported-ID rejection and MM7 title isolation. The private-backed first-map test checks that each authored selector's table row retains its source identity. Native arm64 syntax checks for the modified runtime translation units, a 28/28 read-only dispatcher reconciliation and diff checks passed. The linked headless test and audible playback remain pending integration.

The executable's full seasonal and transition algorithm remains unresolved. The direct terrain accessor and sound dispatcher prove that raw tile identity matters; they do not establish a complete winter or shoreline remap. The separate [tile selector packet](MM6_F4_OUTDOOR_TILE_SELECTORS.md) keeps unchanged MM6 art stable under the current season rule. A later MM6 tile schema can use these retained raw fields to model changed seasons and transitions only after their original behavior is traced.
