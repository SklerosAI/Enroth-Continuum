# MM6 Spell Guide Claims F2 Source

The [spell guide packet](../project/MM6_SPELL_GUIDE_CLAIMS_F2.md) and [probe](../scripts/mm6_probe_spell_guide_claims.py) give all 99 retained MM6 spell IDs a guide heading locator. All 56 spell-section pages were rendered and inspected. Each of the 99 visually checked guide base costs matches the retained MM6 table; 22 differ from inherited MM7 static base costs. Named guide cost, target and damage ambiguities stay in the ignored private matrix. All 99 quantitative rank effects and recovery rules still need reconciliation; a matching base cost does not prove the cast works.
