# MM6 HUD navigation resources

Status: **compass/zoom and four frame candidates; headless resource checks pending; visual acceptance pending** (2026-11-06).

The inherited `SetUserInterface` skin requests many MM7 alignment-specific icons that are absent from the private MM6 icon archive. `UI_Create` likewise requests MM7 tome and status art. The retained archive contains a compass strip and two zoom-labelled controls whose names and dimensions make them plausible HUD candidates. The exact plus/minus meaning remains provisional. `hudNavigationResources(GameTitle, ...)` selects those three MM6 identifiers for every alignment while passing through the exact existing MM7 identifiers. `SetUserInterface` uses the result at its three alignment branches. A headless test checks that all three chosen MM6 entries can be read, and a pure test checks the title selection and exact MM7 neutral, good and evil preservation; neither test establishes zoom semantics.

The owner's original manual, page 30, shows a 640×480 adventure screen with a right panel, bottom portrait strip, top edge and left edge. Four retained MM6 frame images match those regions and dimensions: the right image ends at the bottom of the screen when placed at (468, 141), the portrait strip ends at the bottom at (0, 371), and the top and left edges meet at (0, 0) and (0, 8). `hudFrameAssets(GameTitle, ...)` selects those four images only for MM6, while `hudFramePositions(GameTitle)` selects their matching draw positions. The MM7 assets and positions pass through exactly for neutral, good and evil. Headless checks assert the four MM6 image dimensions and pure checks assert both title branches. No original image bytes are committed.

The right frame has four book images baked into it. The manual identifies four portable books: Quests, Notes, Map and Events. Its next page says the Events Book manages time; the retained MM6 icon archive has a time-oriented book background, and the current Calendar view displays time, day, month, year, moon and location. This supports routing the fourth **input** to the existing Calendar action, while its MM6 page content and art remain unverified.

`UI_Create` now installs four MM6 hit boxes over the baked book region, with matching Quest, Notes, Map and Calendar input actions. It does not request the absent MM7 tome icons or create an MM6 History button. MM7 still constructs its original five image-backed buttons. The MM6 books use no separate overlay or flashing texture until matching art exists; their visible spines remain in the right frame. Headless checks inspect each live button rectangle, action and message, the absence of the fifth button, and title isolation. Synthetic button delivery into a book screen cannot be accepted before the guarded first map is playable.

The MM6 History button pointer is explicitly null. The only route that stores it is the inherited Journal overlay constructor; that constructor does not dereference it, and the overlay's MM6 `Update` returns before accessing its button. The right-panel item's MM6 path also returns before touching the null storyline texture. A source search found no other pointer consumers. This makes the absent fifth button safe at the current shared call sites, while Journal content itself remains an unresolved MM6 route.

The hit boxes are bounded candidates measured against the retained frame; a graphical pass must verify cursor targeting and click feedback. The remaining minimap frame, status art and other skin requests stay explicit presentation blockers. The similarly named MM6 `book` item art belongs to spell-book inventory entries and is not evidence for HUD tomes. Resource presence alone does not establish a usable MM6 in-game interface; no detailed private icon inventory is tracked.

After the save/chest map gates open, a graphical pass must inspect the frame seams, compass crop and heading, zoom button meaning and feedback, all four book controls and their opened pages, and the surrounding HUD at the actual render resolution. Resolve the other icon families from source evidence and then verify them visually before claiming an MM6 HUD.
