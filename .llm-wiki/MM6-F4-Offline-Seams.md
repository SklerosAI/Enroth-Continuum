F4 implementation is underway after the [F3 original-data gate](../project/MM6_F3_GATE_REVIEW.md). The source tree has **partial offline seams**, not a playable campaign. No partial MM6 game was launched or Atlas Play action enabled for these changes.

The integrated native `MM6.app`, MM7 `OpenEnroth.app` and unit targets built on this Mac, and the offline suite passed 178 tests from 56 suites. These cover event framing and selected guarded effects, title-specific class/item/autonote/condition variable checks, indoor/outdoor wire readers and retained projections, `.ec6` field codecs and sixteen-section assembly, private transport/spell, healing, timed-cure and combat-buff rules, First Aid cast preflight, seven monster damage labels, six explicit resistance columns, 93 special-attack labels, 55 monster casts and seven spell damage tags, all 557 retained house type rows, 119 private room descriptors, read-only house service metadata and transport selection, house service and movie-name gates, all 100 award rows, the 193-use award operand check, the 841-cell potion matrix and guarded consumable path, spell-item IDs and school prerequisites, monster descriptor bounds, hireling/NPC lookup and item-icon bounds, F3-verified private map-source/base validation and directory order. Separate F3-matched audits cover all 611 active quest-bit uses, 592 day-of-year comparisons and 147 gold-variable uses. The event fixture includes all 68 active retained EVT resources, the indoor fixture checks all 52 pairs, and the outdoor fixture checks all 15 from ignored local data. The known-save validator exercised all 67 retained base/delta pairs and negative rejection cases. The runner also passed F3-matched spell-item, actor-descriptor, item-footprint, class/school and private 67-name map-manifest checks. The general non-MM6 unit suite passed 438/438 from the same build; creation exporter tests passed separately. Original game data and exact per-ID reports remain ignored under `local-game-data/`.
