# MM6 standard-enchanted equipment projection — F4 detached adapter

Status, 2026-11-06: `stageStandardItem`/`captureStandardItem` form a detached, raw-preserving view of MM6 equipment carrying a standard bonus. The later [bounded inventory stage](MM6_F4_BOUNDED_INVENTORY_PROJECTION.md) combines it with the plain-item adapter; neither joins live Party inventory or opens normal SaveLoad.

The accepted original MM6 item-picker trace writes a one-based standard attribute at item word +4 and positive strength at +8 only on equipment template types 3–11. The pinned [MMExtension item structure](https://github.com/GrayFace/MMExtension/blob/9d73c0ff0482bd231ea522f752f4fe9b26640925/Scripts/Structs/01%20common%20structs.lua#L508-L529) labels these words `Bonus` and `BonusStrength`. The [source-backed bonus map](MM6_F4_STANDARD_BONUS_MAP.md) supplies the 14 MM6 indices and their shared-runtime attributes; it is also the MM6 item-table loader's row order. This adapter requires an explicit MM6 title, a loaded template of equipment type 3–11, a mapped bonus index, positive signed 32-bit strength and zero special-bonus word. It keeps all 28 original bytes next to the runtime `Item`.

Capture revalidates that raw source and template type. It permits another of the 14 MM6 standard attributes, positive strength and identified/broken flag changes. It preserves charges, maximum charges, body location, owner byte, skipped byte and all unknown flag bits. Changed item ID, special enchantment, potion or gold fields, expiry, MM7 lich-jar state, unsupported attribute or changed template type reject. The adapter now also rejects artifact/relic templates: the shared stat reader routes those through an inherited artifact bonus map instead of their standard field. Both import and edit require `100 * strength` and `baseValue + 100 * strength` to fit signed `int`, matching the shared value calculation's arithmetic. This ceiling is a runtime safety condition, not a claim about MM6's intended enchantment balance or a complete bound on every derived stat.

After the rarity and arithmetic guards, focused standard-item, bonus-map and bounded-inventory checks passed **6/6** on a separate arm64 build. The new check covers the exact representable value boundary, one-above rejection at import and edit, a negative template base where the multiplication alone would overflow, and artifact/relic rejection. An earlier monitored MM6 headless test selected a loaded armor template without recording its private ID, staged a synthetic electricity-resistance record, changed its view to poison/body resistance and recaptured the exact raw tail. It passed **1/1** over 65 samples with no visible window or focus change. No native nonzero saved item or on-screen armor-stat behavior was validated.

The [bounded character-inventory stage](MM6_F4_BOUNDED_INVENTORY_PROJECTION.md) now combines this view with the plain/gold view while keeping raw ownership; it remains detached from Party.
